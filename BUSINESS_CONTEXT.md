# Beauty By Joann — Business Context

## Brand Identity

Beauty By Joann is a luxury digital studio for a professional makeup artist serving:
- Film & Television productions
- Commercial shoots
- Editorial campaigns
- Fashion shows
- Creative & SFX projects
- Bridal clients

**Positioning:** Film, Television, Editorial, Commercial, Fashion & Bridal Makeup Artist
**Vibe:** Netflix production studio × Vogue beauty editorial × Fenty Beauty campaign × Modern African luxury brand

## Services

- Film & Television Makeup
- Editorial Makeup
- Commercial Makeup
- Fashion Show Makeup
- Creative & SFX Makeup
- Bridal Specialist

## Brand Mission
*To create professional, camera-ready beauty experiences that bring every story, character, and client vision to life.*

## Brand Vision
*To become one of Africa's most trusted names in film, television, editorial, and luxury beauty artistry.*

## AI Assistant Guidelines

**Identity:** "Joann's Digital Beauty Consultant"
**Personality:** Calm, Patient, Professional, Creative

**NEVER:**
- Invent pricing
- Invent availability
- Invent awards
- Invent certifications

**If unknown:** "I'd recommend contacting Joann directly regarding that."

## Key Design Principles

- Image-first: 70% visual, 30% copy
- Luxury creative studio (not a beauty salon)
- Bridal < 20% of homepage content
- No developer language in UI
- Every look must be traceable to the artist's work — no fabricated content
